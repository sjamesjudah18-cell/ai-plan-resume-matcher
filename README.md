# 🎯 Plan Resume Matcher AI

**An AI-powered resume analysis, job-matching, and career planning platform with an intelligent AI Career Copilot.**

Plan Resume Matcher AI helps job seekers understand how well their resumes align with real-world job descriptions, identify missing skills, improve their resumes, and plan their career paths using AI-powered insights.

## 🚀 Overview

Finding the right job requires more than just having a resume. Candidates need to understand job requirements, identify skill gaps, optimize their resumes, and prepare for interviews.

Plan Resume Matcher AI addresses these challenges through an interactive platform that combines resume parsing, job-description analysis, AI-powered recommendations, and an intelligent chatbot to provide personalized career guidance.

Users can upload their resumes, analyze their compatibility with target roles, explore improvement opportunities, and interact with the AI Career Copilot for career-related assistance.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📄 1. Resume Analysis
- Upload and extract text from PDF resumes.
- Analyze resume content, qualifications, experience, and skills.
- Identify strengths and areas that need improvement.

### 🎯 2. AI-Powered Resume–Job Matching
- Compare resumes against real-world job descriptions.
- Evaluate candidate compatibility with target roles.
- Highlight relevant qualifications and matching skills.
- Help users understand how closely their profiles align with job requirements.

### 🧠 3. Skill Gap Analysis
- Identify skills that match job requirements.
- Highlight missing or underrepresented skills.
- Provide insights into areas that require improvement.
- Help candidates prioritize the skills needed for their target roles.

### 🤖 4. AI Career Copilot
- Interactive AI chatbot for personalized career assistance.
- Answer questions about resumes, job descriptions, and career opportunities.
- Provide guidance on improving resumes and professional profiles.
- Help users understand skill gaps and explore suitable career paths.
- Offer assistance with job preparation and professional development.

### 📈 5. AI-Powered Recommendations
- Generate personalized suggestions based on resume and job requirements.
- Recommend improvements to resume content.
- Help users better communicate their relevant skills and experience.
- Provide actionable guidance for improving job readiness.

### ✅ 6. ATS Optimization
- Analyze resume content against job requirements.
- Identify potentially missing keywords and relevant skills.
- Suggest improvements to make resumes clearer and more relevant to target positions.

### 🗺️ 7. Career Planning
- Help users identify skills to develop for their desired roles.
- Support structured career development planning.
- Connect identified skill gaps with learning priorities and professional goals.

### 💻 8. Interactive Dashboard
- Built with Streamlit for an accessible user experience.
- Organizes resume analysis and matching insights.
- Presents results in a user-friendly interface.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Upload Resume:** The user uploads a resume in PDF format.
2. **Enter Job Description:** The user provides the job description for the desired position.
3. **Extract and Analyze:** The application extracts resume text and analyzes relevant information.
4. **Match and Compare:** Resume details are evaluated against job requirements.
5. **Identify Skill Gaps:** The platform highlights matching skills and potential gaps.
6. **Generate Recommendations:** AI-powered insights help the user identify improvements.
7. **Consult the AI Copilot:** The user can interact with the chatbot for additional career guidance.
